Privacy

This site collects very little. Here's all of it.

Last updated October 8, 2026

Visiting the site

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't set cookies, doesn't use local storage for tracking, and doesn't follow you to other sites. What I see is aggregate: how many page views, which pages, which countries and browsers, roughly how fast pages loaded. I can't see who you are.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps standard server logs to run and protect the service. There are no ads, no social widgets and no other third-party scripts.

The "Latest from the workshop" list on the now page is my own public GitHub activity. Your browser asks this site for it, and this site asks GitHub, so your visit isn't passed on to GitHub.

A few small things are remembered in your own browser: your light or dark theme choice, so the next page matches; for the current tab session only, that you've already seen the boot animation; and the last copy of the workshop list, so the now page can still show it when you're offline. If you sign the guestbook, a copy of your own entry (name, site and message) is also kept in this browser so you can see it while it waits for approval; it's removed once the entry is approved, and after 60 days at most. None of these ever leave your device.

The arcade

The games in the arcade are separate little websites of mine (on *.dillon-eu-green.workers.dev). Nothing from them loads until you press Play; then the game runs in a sandboxed frame on the page, without being told which page it's on. Like when you open a game directly, it may keep your progress and settings in your browser.

The guestbook

When you sign the guestbook, I store what you type: your name, your site address if you give one, and your message. Approved entries are shown publicly, with the date. Your site address is shown as plain text, not as a link.

I also keep the country you signed from, as a two-letter code that Cloudflare works out from your connection and passes along with the request. It's not your IP address, and your IP address isn't stored for it. Countries of approved entries appear on the guestbook only all together, as a "signed from N countries" row of flags, never next to your name.

I also store a salted hash of your IP address (for IPv6, of your network's address block). It's a one-way scramble made with a secret key that isn't stored with the data, so it can't be turned back into your IP. I use it only to limit how many entries can come from one place in an hour, which keeps spam out, and hashes older than two days are erased whenever a new entry comes in.

The studio form

When you fill in the form on the studio page, I store what you type: your email, your role, your organization size if you give it, your answer about your workflow, and whether you asked to be emailed when something ships. Plus the same salted IP hash, for the same reason.

Your email is used only to reply to you or, if you ticked the box, to tell you when something ships. It's never sold or shared, and it isn't added to any marketing list.

Where it's kept

Form entries live in a private Cloudflare D1 database that only I can read. When email alerts are switched on, a copy of each new guestbook entry or studio form answer (never the IP hash) is also emailed to me, so I can read and reply to it. Nothing you type is shown publicly except approved guestbook entries.

Deleting your data

Email dillon@dillongreen.dev and tell me what to remove: a guestbook entry, or your studio form answers (send it from the address you used). I'll delete it and let you know.
