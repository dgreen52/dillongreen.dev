Linework support

Help with Linework on iPad and iPhone. If something here doesn't answer it, email me and I'll get back to you.

Last updated October 19, 2026

Contact

Email dillon@dillongreen.dev. Say which device and iOS version you're on, and what you tapped before it went wrong. A screenshot helps. Please don't send drawings you aren't allowed to share; a description of the file is usually enough.

What it opens

  • DXF: ASCII, R12 to 2018.
  • DGN v7, 2D and 3D.
  • PDF: every page of a PDF is a sheet of its own.

DWG and DGN v8 are not supported. Save those as DXF or DGN v7 first, from the program that made them. A scanned PDF opens and can be marked up and printed, but a scan is a picture, so there are no wires in it to trace.

Getting started

  1. On the home screen, open the sample Control panel: three invented sheets to try everything on.
  2. Tap Scan and point the camera at a wire label such as W101 (written on paper works). Tap the label when it's highlighted: Linework opens the sheet and lights up the wire.
  3. To use your own drawings, Open a folder of sheets from Files (iCloud Drive, On My iPad or iPhone, or a server). The folder becomes one set, with a sheet list and Find across every sheet.

On iPhone, everything is in the bar at the bottom: Library, Trace, Measure, Markup, Scan and More.

Questions

The camera doesn't find my label

The label has to be text in the drawing, not part of a picture. Small misreads like O for 0 or a missing dash are forgiven, but a different number never is. If the camera can't read it, type the label in the box on the "No match" card.

A set I opened before says it can't find the folder

The folder was moved or renamed in Files. Tap Pick the folder again and choose it; your marks come back with it.

Printing to scale

In Print / PDF, pick the sheet size and a scale, then print on paper of that size with the printer's own scaling off. If the printer uses different paper, Linework tells you the print isn't to scale. Save as PDF makes a vector PDF at the same scale.

Where are my markups and check-off marks kept?

On your device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded. They're included in your device backups, and they're deleted if you delete the app. To keep a copy of your check-off, save the wire list (CSV) to Files.

Does it need the internet?

No. Linework works in airplane mode. Keep a set offline to have it with you where the folder itself can't be reached.

Is this the same as Linework on the web?

It's built on Linework. The app adds camera label lookup, sheet sets from Files, an offline library, Apple Pencil markup, printing and PDFs to scale, the wire list and check-off.

Buying for a team

Organizations can buy it in volume through Apple Business Manager. There's no account to set up.

Refunds

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Linework for iPad and iPhone collects no data. The details are in its privacy policy.
