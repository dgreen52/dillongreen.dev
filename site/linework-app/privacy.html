PRIVACY Linework for iPad and iPhone

Linework privacy policy

The app collects no data. Your drawings stay on your device.

Last updated October 19, 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. Linework for iPad and iPhone has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code that collects data. It makes no network requests. The App Store lists it as Data Not Collected.

Your drawings

Linework reads drawings from where you choose them in Files. When you keep a set offline, a copy is stored inside the app on your device. Your markups, check-off marks, the library list and your settings are stored there too. None of it is sent anywhere. It's part of your device backups like other app data, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

Files you save or print (PNG, PDF, the wire list) go only where you send them.

The camera

The camera is used only to read the text of a wire label, on the device, with Apple's own text recognition. Camera frames and photos are not saved and not sent anywhere. The app doesn't ask for access to your photo library.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports. They don't include your drawings. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so, with a new date above, before the update that changes it.
