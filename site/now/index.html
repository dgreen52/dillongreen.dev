What I'm doing now

Last updated October 3, 2026 · Seattle, WA

    Simulator engineering on Boeing 737 and 787 and Airbus A330 full flight simulators: keeping the devices qualified, healthy and ready for the next crew. Most of my ideas for tools start there.

    • Crumb In App Store review

      Recipe cleaner, cookbook and weekly meal planner. The iPhone app is with Apple; the web version is up in the meantime.

    • pocket429 Live on the web

      ARINC 429 decoder for avionics technicians, live at pocket429.dillongreen.dev. The iOS app is being prepared for submission.

  3. 03Building

    • Fernwood Main focus · Playable preview

      A cozy farm-and-explore RPG, aiming for Steam first. Polishing Spring into a demo.

      A rhythm street racer where the music is the race: drive to the beat and the song fills in.

    • Little Airfield Playable prototype

      A cozy airport builder, heading for an iPhone build next.

    • Dragon Realm Online Live

      A real-time multiplayer browser game, recently renamed from jaderealm.

    • This site In progress

      Adding live status for my apps, gameplay clips on the game cards, and share cards for every page.

    Talking to aviation folks about the manual workflows that should be tools: the spreadsheets, binders and retyped numbers that eat a shift. If that's you, the studio page has a short form.
