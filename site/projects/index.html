    Mixtape Drift

    A cute pixel street racer where the music is the race: drive to the beat and the song fills in.

    • WebAudio
    • Canvas
    • Rhythm
    • Pixel art
    Play
    Read more

    Drums and bass always play; clean driving, near misses, on-beat boosts and drifts released on the beat bring in keys, lead and sparkle, and a bump drops a layer. Cruise (endless, collect cassettes), Sprint (race three rivals through one song, GO on the downbeat) and Groove, plus a Garage for shape, paint, underglow and decals. Three original songs played by a small WebAudio synth band composed in code, a music clock driving the beat timing, a low-res scanline road renderer and pixel art drawn in code, with pure rules, track and composer modules under Node tests and Playwright browser checks.

    Smart Mirror

    A Raspberry Pi magic mirror with a cyberpunk streak, run by a zero-dependency Python server.

    • Raspberry Pi
    • Python stdlib
    • Server-sent events
    • PIR sensor
    Read more

    A monitor behind two-way acrylic and a browser UI in a self-starting Chromium kiosk. Weather, calendar, news and household notes posted from phones by QR code, with live push. It greets whoever is home by pinging their phones on Wi-Fi, so nothing leaves the LAN, and a PIR sensor wakes the screen. Easter eggs: a MIRROR-OS boot, katakana rain when it is actually raining, and a fake netrunner intrusion.
